# CS321

## Textbook Finder
Students, meet TextbookFinder, a Discord Bot that finds the cheapest textbook options for you in seconds! Instead of crowding your browser and spending hours finding cheap and real listings, just type a book title or ISBN in TextbookFinder. The bot verifies you’re looking at the book you need for class, and shows you a ranked list of the top cheapest buy/rent options without misleading listings.
